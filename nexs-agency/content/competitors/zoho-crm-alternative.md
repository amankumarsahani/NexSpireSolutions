## 1. Why people look for a Zoho CRM alternative

Zoho CRM is cheap for what it includes, and that's real — but reviewers consistently flag the same two things: the interface feels dated next to newer tools, and getting it to actually match your business's workflow means custom modules, workflow rules, and often Deluge scripting. For a small team without a dedicated admin, that configuration burden is the real cost, even when the license price is low.

## 2. NapCRM as the alternative — quick summary

NapCRM starts from the opposite direction: instead of a highly configurable blank slate, it ships with the industry workflow already built — 14 verticals including real estate, healthcare, legal, salon, and hospitality. You're picking a template close to your business on day one instead of building one.

## 3. Detailed comparison

| | NapCRM | Zoho CRM |
|---|---|---|
| Entry price | $49/mo (Starter, flat) | ~$14–20/user/mo (Standard) |
| Industry templates | 14 built-in verticals | None — generic + Deluge scripting to customize |
| Setup effort | Pick industry template, configure limits | Build modules/workflows from scratch |
| Suite integration | Standalone (CRM + storefront) | Deep tie-in to Zoho One (Books, Desk, Campaigns) |
| UI/UX | Modern | Frequently cited by reviewers as dated |
| Pricing scale | Flat per-tier, capped seats/records | Per-user, scales linearly with headcount |
| Data isolation | Dedicated DB per tenant | Shared multi-tenant |
| E-commerce/storefront | Built-in | Separate Zoho Commerce product |

**Where the real trade-off is:** Zoho CRM is cheaper per seat at small headcounts and wins outright if you're already running Zoho Books, Desk, or Campaigns — the suite integration is a genuine advantage NapCRM doesn't have. NapCRM wins on time-to-value: no Deluge scripting, no building pipelines from zero, because the vertical template already reflects your industry.

## 4. Who should switch

Switch if: you tried configuring Zoho CRM for your industry and found yourself building custom modules and workflow rules for weeks, or you're not using the rest of the Zoho suite so the suite discount doesn't apply to you anyway.

Don't switch if: you're already deep in Zoho One (Books/Desk/Campaigns) — the cross-product data flow you'd lose is a real cost, and NapCRM doesn't have an equivalent connected suite.

## 5. Migration path

- Export leads/contacts/deals via Zoho's standard CSV export
- Custom modules built in Zoho don't map 1:1 — during onboarding, Napnix maps your data into the closest matching NapCRM industry template fields, and any Zoho-specific custom modules that don't have a NapCRM equivalent get flagged for a custom-build conversation (this is where the "agency behind the CRM" model helps — a true field-for-field gap can become a feature request, not a dead end)
- Zoho workflow automations and Deluge scripts do not transfer — expect to rebuild automation logic using NapCRM's workflow engine
