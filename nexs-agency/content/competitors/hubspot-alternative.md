## 1. Why people look for a HubSpot alternative

HubSpot's free CRM is a genuinely good on-ramp — which is exactly why the jump hurts. Teams get used to the platform, their contact list grows, and then the cost of moving into Marketing/Sales Hub Professional (commonly **$800+/month** once seats and contact tiers stack up) shows up right as the team is trying to prove ROI, not defend a bigger software line item.

The other recurring complaint: HubSpot's CRM object model is generic by design. It doesn't know if you sell real estate, run a salon, or manage legal cases — you build that structure yourself with custom properties and pipelines, every time, from scratch.

## 2. NapCRM as the alternative — quick summary

NapCRM skips the free-tier-to-cliff pricing model. Every tier is a flat, published rate ($49–$99/mo, custom above that), and — unlike HubSpot's blank CRM — NapCRM ships with **14 industry-specific templates** already built: pipelines, fields, and workflows shaped for real estate, healthcare, legal, salon, hospitality, and more. You're not paying for a generic object model and then spending implementation time turning it into your workflow.

**Pricing that matters in practice:** a 5-person team on NapCRM Growth is $79/mo flat. The same team hitting HubSpot's Professional tier, once contact volume crosses a few thousand, is routinely 5–10x that — before any add-ons.

**Where HubSpot still wins:** if inbound marketing content, SEO tooling, and email sequencing are the core job, HubSpot's marketing depth is real and mature. NapCRM's marketing feature set (landing pages, WhatsApp, workflows) is functional but shallower — it's a CRM with marketing features, not a marketing platform with a CRM bolted on.

## 4. Who should switch

Switch if: you're a service business (not primarily inbound-marketing-led), you know your industry's workflow already and don't want to rebuild it in a generic tool, or you've seen your HubSpot bill jump after a tier change.

Don't switch if: your team's core function is content/inbound marketing and you rely on HubSpot's SEO, blogging, and sequencing tools daily — NapCRM won't replace that layer.

## 5. Migration path

- Export contacts/deals from HubSpot (native CSV export)
- Map to the relevant NapCRM industry template's fields (Napnix onboarding maps this during setup, since there's no self-serve import wizard yet — flag this honestly, it's a real gap vs. HubSpot's guided import)
- Historical email/engagement data does not migrate — plan to archive it in HubSpot or export separately if needed for records
